The problems we keep meeting

The member register is paper

Exercise books at the branch. A spreadsheet at headquarters that never matches them. When a secretary leaves, years of records leave with them. Nobody can say with confidence how many members you actually have.

Dues you cannot trace

Who has paid this year? Who is owing? Which branch collected what? Answering means chasing branch officers for figures, then arguing over totals that do not add up.

Duplicate and ghost members

The same person registered twice in two wards. Names on the list nobody can produce. Inflated member counts make every decision — budgets, elections, allocations — harder to trust.

Verification takes days

Someone presents themselves as a member and you have no fast way to confirm it. Calls to the branch, digging through files, waiting on one person who knows where the book is.

What your system — and its agent — does about it

A registration portal members use themselves

An association member registration portal built around your structure. Members register once and get a permanent membership ID in your format — tied to state, LGA, ward, branch or chapter, whatever your organization uses. They can print a membership slip and update their own details, so nobody queues at the secretariat for a phone number change.

One member record, no duplicates

Every member lives in one database with duplicate control at registration — same person, same phone, same details get caught before they become a second record. Your staff ask the assistant on WhatsApp in plain language: how many members registered in this branch this month? The answer comes from the records, not from memory.

Verification in seconds

Look any member up by ID, name or phone and confirm their status on the spot. When staff update a record, the system shows them exactly what will change and asks them to confirm before it saves. Every change is logged — who did it, when, and what it was before.

Dues tracking and reports leadership can trust

Record dues as they come in, per member and per branch. The system sends reminders to whoever should follow up, and leadership gets regular reports — registrations, payments, outstanding dues — without anyone compiling a spreadsheet by hand.

Real build, running today

This is not our first member register. We built a nationwide membership registration portal for a large national organization: members register once and receive a permanent membership ID tied to their state, LGA and ward; they log in to a dashboard showing their ID, membership category and registration date; the portal prints a temporary membership slip while permanent cards are processed; and members update their own details instead of queuing at an office. That build is proof of range, not a product on a shelf — your cooperative, association or union would get a system designed around your own branch structure, your ID format, and how your dues actually work.
