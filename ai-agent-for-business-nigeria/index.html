The problems we keep meeting

Your business lives in scattered records

Sales in a notebook, stock in a spreadsheet, staff updates buried in WhatsApp groups. You cannot see the whole business in one place — and when someone leaves, their knowledge leaves with them.

Reports eat your evenings

At the end of the day, someone gathers numbers from phones and paper, types them up, and hopes nothing was missed. By the time the report is ready, the moment to act on it has passed.

The AI you have tried only talks

Chatbots answer customer questions — that is a receptionist. Nobody has built you the thing that runs the back office: records, stock, money, reminders, daily reports.

Problems surface late

Low stock, an unusual expense, cash that does not add up — you find out days later, when it has already cost you money. Nobody is watching the numbers between reports.

What your system — and its agent — does about it

Staff report by WhatsApp message

"Sold 40 crates to Alhaji Musa at ₦6,500" is a complete report. The agent reads it, prepares the entry — 40 crates × ₦6,500 = ₦260,000, batch, date — and asks for confirmation. Staff can write in English, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, or Pidgin. No forms, no new app to learn.

Nothing saves without a human yes

The agent drafts every entry and shows it to a person first — preview and confirm. If the draft is wrong, your staff cancels it and nothing touches your books. Every saved action is logged with who confirmed it and when.

Reports compile themselves

Dashboards update the moment an entry is confirmed. Daily summaries arrive without anyone typing them up, and you can simply ask — "how many crates have we sold today?" — and get the answer on the spot.

It watches, reminds, and stays in its lane

Between reports, the agent keeps an eye on the operation — flagging things like low feed or unusual costs — and handles routine reminders so nothing depends on memory. It follows a playbook you approve: what happens by itself, and what waits for a human yes. All of it built around how your business runs, not a template.

Questions owners ask

Is an AI agent for business just a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions — a receptionist. An agent works inside your operations system: it records what staff report, compiles reports, sends reminders, and flags risks. It drafts, you confirm, and every action is logged. The two can even work side by side — a chatbot greets your customers while the agent runs the back office.

Can AI really work for a small business in Nigeria?

Yes. If you have staff, stock, sales, or members to keep track of, you are big enough to lose money to scattered records. We build in stages, starting with the part that hurts most, and your staff use WhatsApp — the app they already have. The free readiness check tells you honestly where you stand; sometimes the honest answer is "not yet", and we will say so.

How does WhatsApp business automation work if my staff are not technical?

If they can send a WhatsApp message, they can use the system. Staff write in plain language — English, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, or Pidgin — and the agent turns the message into a proper record once they confirm it. No forms, no training manuals, no new app for frontline staff. The dashboards are there for whoever wants the full picture.
